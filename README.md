# HR-Dashboard

1. Project Title / Headline 

 HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Dashboard – Power BI


2. Short Description / Purpose

 This HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report designed to analyze employee workforce data and understand employee attrition, active employees,     demographics, departments, education, job roles, and performance ratings.

 To analyze employee attrition and workforce trends effectively.
 To identify attrition patterns based on age, gender, department, education and job role.
 To monitor key HR KPIs such as total employees, active employees, attrition count and attrition rate.
 To provide an interactive and easy-to-understand HR analytics solution for data-driven workforce analysis.


3. Tools & Technologies Used

 🔷 Power BI Desktop – Dashboard development & interactive visualization.
 🔄 Power Query – Data cleaning, transformation & preparation.
 📐 DAX – Measures, KPIs & calculated columns.
 🗂️ Data Modeling – Relationships and structured data analysis.
 📊 Power BI Visuals – Cards, Donut Charts, Column Charts & Matrix.
 🎛️ Slicers & Filters – Interactive dashboard analysis.
 📈 Data Analysis – Attrition, demographics, departments & performance insights.


4. Data Source
 
 📄 HR Employee Dataset – Employee demographics, job details, department, education, performance ratings and attrition-related data.

5. Features / Highlights

 🔴 Business Problem
  High employee attrition can increase recruitment costs, workload, and productivity loss.
  HR teams need a clear view of where and among whom employee attrition is occurring.
  Raw employee data makes it difficult to identify patterns across departments, age groups, gender, education, and job roles.

 🎯 Goal of the Dashboard
 Provide a single interactive view of key HR metrics.
 Track employee attrition, active employees, and attrition rate.
 Identify patterns and segments associated with employee attrition.
 Support data-driven HR workforce analysis and planning.

 📊 Key Visuals – Brief Walkthrough
 KPI Cards: Display Total Employees, Attrition Count, Attrition Rate, and Active Employees.
 Job Role Slicer: Allows users to filter the dashboard by specific job roles.
 Department Chart: Compares employee attrition across R&D, Sales, and HR.
 Age Histogram: Shows the distribution of employees across different age groups.
 Education Donut Chart: Displays attrition across different education fields.
 Age & Gender Donut Chart: Provides a combined view of attrition by age band and gender.
 Performance Matrix: Shows performance ratings across different job roles.

 💼 Business Impact
 Helps HR teams monitor workforce health through centralized KPIs.
 Enables identification of departments and employee segments with higher attrition.
 Supports better workforce planning and retention analysis.
 Reduces dependency on manual reporting by providing an interactive analytical dashboard.

💡 Key Insights
 237 employees are recorded as having left the organization.
 The overall attrition rate is 16.12%.
 1,233 employees are currently active out of 1,470 total employees.
 R&D has the highest attrition count among the displayed departments.
 The dashboard shows noticeable differences in attrition across age bands, gender, and education fields.
 Performance ratings vary across job roles, enabling further analysis of role-wise workforce performance.
